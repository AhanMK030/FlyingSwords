# Human Sovereign Banner

Raise a soul-bound banner and awaken its six ancient seals through battle. **Human Sovereign Banner** is a growing artifact included in the **Flying Swords** mod for **Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous**, with a custom weapon model, painted inventory icon, native Greatclub attack animations, and increasingly intense black mist created with Unity particles.

## How to obtain it

Install the main Flying Swords archive through **Unity Mod Manager**, with **TabletopTweaks-Core** installed. Load your save and open **UMM → Flying Swords → Claim Initial Human Sovereign Banner**.

The button grants **one initial +0 banner per save**. It is an acquisition window, rather than a stage-testing menu: advanced-stage grant buttons have been removed. Existing saves that already own a progressing banner or have unlocked its seals are recognized as having claimed it. Claiming the banner grants no soul essence and unlocks no powers.

The banner is obtained through UMM, rather than from a merchant. All required models, effects, icons and runtime assets are included in the main mod archive; no separate animation package is needed.

## A weapon that grows with your campaign

The banner is a **cold-iron, two-handed melee Greatclub**, dealing **1d12 bludgeoning damage** with an initial **20/×3** critical profile and **5-foot reach**. It uses the game's Greatclub proficiency, weapon feats and weapon-training rules.

Keep the progressing banner in the **party inventory or a party member's equipment** when defeating the bosses below. Any party member may land the killing blow; the banner's wielder does not need to score the kill. Storage in a shared stash alone does not qualify.

**Boss spoilers follow.**

| Seal | Required boss and location | Weapon growth | Power unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Large Water Elemental** — Shield Maze, Prologue | +1 enhancement | **Elemental Body:** immunity to poison, sleep, paralysis, stun, critical hits, precision damage, flanking and cold. |
| 2 | **Nabasu** — the optional encounter in Market Square, Act 1 | +2 enhancement; critical threat becomes 19–20/×3 | **Soul Ward:** party kills add temporary HP equal to the defeated enemy's CR, rounded down, minimum 1. Accumulates up to 50% of the wielder's maximum HP; cleared by a full rest. |
| 3 | **Blightmaw** — Drezen Citadel, Act 2 | +3 enhancement; reach increases to 10 feet | **Unending Blood Battle:** each party kill grants +1 to banner attack rolls and damage, stacking up to +3. Cleared by a full rest. |
| 4 | **Playful Darkness** — Midnight Fane, Act 3 | +4 enhancement | **Spell Theft:** the first successful hit each round steals one random eligible, dispellable beneficial spell effect from the target, retaining its remaining duration. Permanent, mythic, area and story effects are excluded. |
| 5 | **Melazmera**, the umbral dragon — Colyphyr Mines, Act 4 | +5 enhancement; critical threat becomes 18–20/×3 | **Bone Erosion:** each hit deals 1 additional point of Constitution ability damage, respecting the target's ability-damage immunities. |
| 6 | **Ashen Lady** — her final combat form in the Ineluctable Prison, Act 5 | Retains +5 enhancement, 18–20/×3 criticals and 10-foot reach | **All Souls Bow:** the wielder gains a unique +4 bonus to all six ability scores. Stacks with other sources; multiple banners do not stack. |

Unlocks are shared by the save and persist through rest. The highest unlocked seal determines the weapon's statistics, but **each power requires its own boss**: skipping an earlier boss does not grant that boss's power. Kills made before the party carries the progressing banner are not retroactively credited. Core difficulty is not required by the mod's unlock rules.

## Soul essence and enchantments

Eligible party kills yield **soul essence equal to floor(CR), minimum 1** while the party carries a banner. Each enemy is counted once; allied units, summons and ineligible manufactured encounters are excluded. Essence is shared by the save and remains after rest.

- **Refine Souls into Gold:** exchange 1 essence for 50 gold, with presets for 1, 10, 50, 100 or all available essence.
- **Infusion Enchantments:** open one compact native menu containing all 18 enchantment toggles. Select up to one enchantment at +1/+2, two at +3/+4, or three at +5. Click an active choice again to deselect it.
- **Infuse Souls into the Banner:** confirm the selection and pay its complete essence cost. Replacing an existing combination costs the full price again. Infusions last until the next full rest.
- **Clear Infusion Selection:** cancel pending choices without spending essence or removing an already applied infusion.

| Essence per enchantment | Available choices |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | Flaming, Frost, Shock, Corrosive, Thundering, Ghost Touch, Heartseeker |
| 40 | Flaming Burst, Icy Burst, Shocking Burst, Corrosive Burst, Holy, Unholy, Axiomatic, Anarchic |
| 60 | Bane (Evil Outsider), Speed |
| 80 | Brilliant Energy |

Duplicate choices and a basic elemental enchantment combined with its matching burst are not allowed. These infusions use the game's existing enchantment effects. There is no additional combined +10-equivalent cap imposed by the mod. Current essence is shown in the gold-conversion and infusion-confirmation tooltips.

## Appearance and language

The banner begins without mist. At +1/+2 it emits faint black smoke, at +3/+4 the smoke becomes dense, and at +5 intense black mist swirls around the banner. Its model follows native two-handed Greatclub attacks.

Chinese and English text are selected automatically from the game's language setting; other languages use English.
